My Research Agenda

My research examines how human–AI collaboration can be conceptualised, designed, and assessed in second language learning. It addresses a central educational challenge: GenAI can improve students’ performance without necessarily fostering their development.

Across second language argumentative writing and digital multimodal composing, my work focuses on three interconnected strands:

  • Human-centred design for AI-mediated writing
  • Human–AI interaction processes in writing
  • Process-oriented writing assessment in AI contexts

Projects

My projects include the design and evaluation of a Dynamic Assessment-informed AI writing agent, two university collaborations with Shenzhen University and Southeast University, and an industry–university project with Evoke AI Limited, a Hong Kong AI startup. Together, these projects explore adaptive AI dialogue, AI-supported writing systems, and digital multimodal composing, where learners combine text, images, and other modes to create meaning.
