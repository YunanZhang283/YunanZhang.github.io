My Research Agenda

My research examines how human–AI collaboration can be conceptualised, designed, and assessed in second language learning. My work spans argumentative writing and digital multimodal composing, where learners combine text, images, and other modes to create meaning.

My research connects three strands: designing AI support around learners’ needs and development; understanding how learners engage with AI; and assessing learning and development through evidence from the interaction process. Findings inform the redesign of AI support, forming an iterative design–process–assessment research agenda.

This work has led to nine articles in peer-reviewed SSCI Q1 journals, two manuscripts under revision, and one invited book chapter under review. I have also presented at 12 major international conferences and given an invited talk at UCL.
