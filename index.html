My Research Agenda

My research examines how human–AI collaboration can be conceptualised, designed, and assessed in second language learning.

Across both monomodal argumentative writing and digital multimodal composing (i.e., literacy practices that combine multiple modes to create meaning), my work focuses on three interconnected strands:

  • Human-centred design for AI-mediated writing: designing AI support around learners’ needs and development.
  • Human–AI interaction processes in writing: understanding how learners engage with AI.
  • Process-oriented assessment in AI contexts: assessing learning and development through evidence from human–AI interaction processes.

Together, these strands form an iterative design–process–assessment research agenda.

This work has led to nine published/accepted articles in SSCI Q1 journals, two manuscripts under revision at SSCI Q1 journals, and one invited book chapter under review. I have also presented at 12 major international conferences and given an invited talk at UCL.
